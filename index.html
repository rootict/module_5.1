PROMPT ANATOMY: PART 1

Task & Context

Defining WHAT to do and WHY you need it.

🎯

The Task

The Action Verb

What exactly do you want the AI to do? Always start with a strong action verb.

// Weak: "about computers"

// Strong: "Explain how a computer CPU works."
📖

The Context

The Background Info

Why are you asking? Who is this for? Giving background helps AI tailor the answer.

"Explain how a computer CPU works to a 10-year-old child who loves LEGO."

Setting boundaries and shaping the final output.

The Rules

5.3 COMPARING PROMPTS

The Power of Context

See how the AI responds to Weak vs. Strong Prompts.

⚠️ Without Context (Weak)
Write an email asking for a day off.
"Hey boss,

I am not coming to work tomorrow because I need a day off. See you later."
✅ With Context (Strong)
Write an email to my corporate manager asking for a day off tomorrow due to a sudden medical checkup. Keep it professional.
"Dear [Manager's Name],

Please accept this request for a day of leave tomorrow due to an unforeseen medical appointment. I will ensure my pending tasks are covered."
